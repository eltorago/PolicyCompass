# Access and staff security policy

Fictional document for the PolicyCompass demonstration. Version 1.0.
Owner: Head of Technology. Approver: Chief Executive. Review: every 12 months.

## Purpose and scope

This policy sets the rules for giving people access to business systems and
preparing them to use those systems safely. It applies to employees, contractors,
temporary staff and service providers working from an office or remotely.
System owners apply these rules to hosted services as well as systems operated
by the organisation.

## Joining, changing roles and leaving

The people team checks a person's identity and completes the screening required
for their role before requesting an account. Managers record the business reason,
systems required and proposed end date in an access request. The system owner
approves the request before the service desk creates the account.

Each person receives a unique user account. Access permissions are limited to the
tasks required for the person's current duties. Managers notify the service desk
before a person changes roles or leaves. The service desk removes superseded
permissions when a role changes and disables departing users' accounts at the
agreed departure time. Urgent departures are handled immediately on instruction
from the people team. Contractors' accounts expire on the agreed contract end date.

System owners review user access every quarter and record the permissions they
retain, change or remove. The service desk investigates accounts with no named
owner and suspends unused accounts after checking service dependencies.

## Authentication and administration

Multi-factor authentication protects remote access, privileged accounts and
internet-facing business services. Users keep authentication factors separate
from their passwords and report unexpected approval requests to the service desk.
Password resets require an identity check through an approved channel.

Administrators use separate privileged accounts for administrative work and
standard accounts for email and web browsing. Privileged access is granted for a
specific task and limited period. The technology team records administrative
activity and reviews emergency account use after each activation. Service accounts
have a named owner, restricted permissions and credentials held in an approved
secrets store. Staff must not share passwords or approve another person's login.

## Remote work and acceptable use

Staff connect through the approved remote access service using managed devices.
Devices must have current security updates, disk encryption and a screen lock.
Staff use approved business storage and keep work material out of personal email
and personal cloud accounts. Lost devices and suspected account compromise must
be reported immediately. Managers arrange alternative work where a device cannot
meet the requirements.

## Awareness and role training

All personnel complete cyber security awareness training during induction and
annually thereafter. Training covers suspicious messages, safe handling of
information, authentication, remote work and reporting incidents. Managers follow
up overdue training and arrange accessible alternatives where needed.

Administrators, developers and incident responders complete additional training
for their responsibilities before working independently. The people team keeps
attendance and completion records. The security team reviews recurring mistakes
and exercise results each quarter to decide which topics need further coaching.

## Exceptions and review

The system owner records requests for temporary exceptions, the business need,
extra safeguards, responsible person and expiry date. The Head of Technology
approves exceptions after a security review and checks them monthly until closed.
The policy owner reviews this policy annually and after a serious access incident.
