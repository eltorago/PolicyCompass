# Supplier and cloud service security policy

Fictional document for the PolicyCompass demonstration. Version 1.0.
Owner: Procurement Manager. Approver: Chief Executive. Review: every 12 months.

## Purpose and scope

This policy sets security requirements for buying and managing products and
services. It applies where a supplier accesses business systems, handles business
information or supports an important service. Purchasing a subscription or trial
with a payment card does not remove the need for a security review.

## Before purchase

The business sponsor describes the proposed service, information involved and
consequences of an outage or compromise. Procurement records suppliers in a
central register, with a business owner and a rating based on access, sensitivity
and service criticality. Higher-risk suppliers require review by security,
privacy, records and legal staff before the organisation commits to the service.

The security assessment checks the supplier's access controls, vulnerability
handling, backup arrangements, incident response and use of subcontractors.
Reviewers examine relevant independent assurance reports and record unresolved
findings. They confirm where information will be stored and processed and who can
administer the service. A certificate alone does not close an identified risk.

## Agreements and responsibilities

Contracts define the information the supplier can use, the permitted purposes
and the responsibilities retained by the organisation. Security requirements
cover access, encryption, incident notification, recovery support and evidence
needed for review. Material changes to hosting, subcontracting or administrative
access require notice and review before taking effect.

Critical suppliers must maintain business continuity arrangements and take part
in agreed recovery exercises. Agreements specify notification contacts, the
information needed during an incident and the time allowed to report suspected
compromise. The business owner keeps a record of agreed service levels, recovery
targets and escalation contacts.

## Access and ongoing review

Supplier access uses named accounts, multi-factor authentication and permissions
limited to approved tasks. Remote administrative access is enabled for the agreed
work period and logged. The system owner reviews supplier accounts quarterly and
removes access when work finishes or personnel change.

Business owners review critical suppliers at least annually and after a serious
incident or material service change. Reviews consider assurance findings, service
performance, security incidents and outstanding remediation. Procurement tracks
actions to closure and refers unacceptable residual risk to the accountable
executive. The security team includes supplier dependencies in incident and
recovery planning.

## Ending or replacing a service

Before signing, the business owner records an exit plan covering data export,
service continuity, credentials and records retention. At termination, the owner
checks that required data has been returned in a usable form and that supplier
access and integrations have been removed. The supplier confirms deletion of
remaining copies when contractual retention and legal holds permit it.

## Exceptions and review

Exceptions require a documented risk assessment, an accountable executive's
approval, temporary safeguards and an expiry date. The Procurement Manager
reviews this policy annually with security and business representatives and uses
supplier incidents and exit exercises to improve the purchasing process.
